# Core Play Loop

> [[00 Start Here|Home]] › [[01 Design Guide|Design Guide]] › **01 Vision** · Previous: [[Player Fantasy]] · Next: [[Session and Presentation]]

## Question

What does a player repeatedly do during a normal session?

## Answer

1. **Choose an intention:** respond to a rumor or event, take a request, improve the home, or pursue a personal goal.
2. **Go out:** explore, meet people, gather, solve a local problem, trade, or fight.
3. **Return and convert:** recover safely at home, use discoveries to craft, build, fulfill needs, gain reputation, and prepare new possibilities.
4. **Leave a trace:** the home, a relationship, a route, or the state of a place changes.

A short session completes one useful task. A longer session links several tasks into an expedition or local story.

The **activity journal** makes that intention explicit. Players open it with J or its visible button, browse the stories and livelihoods their world has revealed, and pin one activity to their own objective card. Automatic guidance remains the default and can be restored at any time. A pin is remembered with the player's identity in that world; it never assigns a quest owner, hides other interactions, or changes a companion's guidance. Completed stories remain readable, and newly unlocked activities appear as shared progress changes. The first journal covers the existing home, welcome, ruins, Nima, Moonwell, supper, Reedbank, food, fishing, furnishing, sunwheat, and festival content.

Opening the journal releases the pointer and reserves local movement, camera, and gameplay actions for the panel; the shared world continues running. It is navigation and personal guidance, not a pause or protection from danger. Closing it restores normal controls, and furnishing and journal panels cannot both be active.

The repaired cottage is the first reliable safe place in this loop. A standing injured player may rest there to recover fully without consuming expedition supplies or advancing the shared clock. Rest is personal and never interrupts other players; trail provisions remain the limited way to recover health while away from home.
