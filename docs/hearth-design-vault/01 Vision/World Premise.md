# World Premise

> [[00 Start Here|Home]] › [[01 Design Guide|Design Guide]] › **01 Vision** · Next: [[Player Fantasy]]

The game takes place in a large, living fantasy world where players are free to explore, build a home, meet different people, and choose the lives they want to create together.

![[Assets/World Poll/01 Magic Fantasy World.png|700]]

## The World

The world is lush, green, and filled with elves, magic, monsters, villages, ancient ruins, forests, rivers, mountains, and distant lands waiting to be explored. Magic and fantasy creatures are normal parts of this world, but they are not the main conflict of the story.

The world should feel inhabited and active. Cities and villages are filled with people who have their own work, needs, relationships, and problems. Beyond them are wild areas, forgotten places, and opportunities for adventure.

## The Players

The player characters come from a homeland ravaged by a long war. They leave that life behind and arrive together in a large fantasy city where many different peoples live and work.

They begin with little money and no reputation. They are not chosen heroes, famous warriors, or important leaders. They are newcomers trying to build better lives and find their places in an unfamiliar city.

## Making a New Life

The players' larger goal is to make names for themselves. They can take jobs, help local people, explore the lands outside the city, join groups, start businesses, build homes, and make friends or enemies.

Making a name does not require combat. One player might become known as a fighter or explorer, while another becomes respected as a farmer, builder, trader, cook, craftsperson, or helpful member of the community. Each player should be able to choose what success means for their character.

As the players grow, the city and the surrounding world begin to recognize them. The homes they build, the people they help, the places they discover, and the choices they make become part of the shared history of their world.

## Tone and Visual Direction

The world should feel colorful, welcoming, and full of natural beauty, while still containing danger and mystery. Peaceful daily life, exciting adventures, and unexpected chaos can all happen in the same world.

The main story is about leaving war behind, starting again, and growing from unknown newcomers into people who have built lives, relationships, and reputations of their own.
