# Player Fantasy

> [[00 Start Here|Home]] › [[01 Design Guide|Design Guide]] › **01 Vision** · Previous: [[World Premise]] · Next: [[Core Play Loop]]

## Question

Who is the player, and what is their central fantasy?

## Answer

The player is an ordinary newcomer rebuilding life after war—not a chosen hero. Their fantasy is to become **known for a life they chose**: explorer, protector, farmer, builder, cook, trader, mage, or a mixture.

Recognition is visible. NPCs remember deeds, places change, the home displays achievements, and the group develops its own history. Success means belonging and leaving a mark, not defeating one final villain.
