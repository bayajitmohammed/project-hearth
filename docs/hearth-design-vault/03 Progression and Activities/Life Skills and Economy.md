# Life Skills and Economy

> [[00 Start Here|Home]] › [[01 Design Guide|Design Guide]] › **03 Progression and Activities** · Previous: [[Shared World Progression]] · Next: [[PvP and Party Activities]]

## Connected activities

Gathering, farming, fishing, and exploration provide materials. Cooking and crafting turn them into expedition supplies, tools, trade goods, decorations, and project contributions. Building creates useful spaces that improve those activities. Settlement needs provide changing reasons to produce different goods.

Each step should make a visible difference; the game avoids long processing chains and inventory micromanagement.

## Loot, trade, and currency

Loot favors recipes, unusual materials, tools with trade-offs, keepsakes, and cosmetics over constant numeric upgrades. Players can trade directly and contribute to shared storage.

Local NPC markets use bounded supply and demand. Coin pays for services, land, travel, and common goods; reputation unlocks access but is not spent. There is no global auction house. Repairs, projects, services, and decoration keep money useful without punishing upkeep.
