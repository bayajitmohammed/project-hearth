# PvP and Party Activities

> [[00 Start Here|Home]] › [[01 Design Guide|Design Guide]] › **03 Progression and Activities** · Previous: [[Life Skills and Economy]] · Next section: [[Design Questions]]

## Optional PvP

There is no hostile open-world PvP. Players opt into arenas, races, contests, or festival games. The standard mode normalizes combat power and supplies equipment; an optional chaos mode allows personal gear. Rewards are reputation, cosmetics, and modest materials—not exclusive power.

## Party activities

Short 3–10 minute activities live at real places: tavern games, fishing contests, cooking rushes, races, duels, seasonal festivals, and cooperative town tasks. They use the same characters and world rewards.

Activities appear as breaks inside normal play and social gatherings, not as a separate lobby or the main progression grind.

## First normalized sparring circle

After the produce stall opens, a small circle southeast of the arrival road accepts two explicit volunteers with E at its sign. The first waits, may cancel by interacting again, or leave freely; the second opt-in places both at equal starting positions and begins a three-second countdown. Other players remain spectators and cannot deal or receive match hits. This is an optional two-person contest, never a gate on solo progress.

Each fighter has three separate match pips. Space and R both perform the same one-pip training tap within two metres, with a 0.7-second recovery; F guards the next tap for 0.5 seconds with a two-second cooldown. Personal loadout, mastery, world injuries and provisions do not change these values. The first fighter to remove the opponent's pips wins; a 60-second time limit ends an unfinished bout as a draw. Movement remains the shared normal movement rule. World health, inventory, mastery and downed state never change because of a sparring hit.

Both finishers of a decisive bout earn one cosmetic sparring ribbon; the latest winner and ribbons persist without power, coin or repeatable world-progression rewards. Leaving the circle, disconnecting or becoming downed cancels without a win or reward. Signup, countdown, pips, attack/guard timers and active rounds are transient and reset safely on load. Replays require two new opt-ins. Ranked matchmaking, teams, spectator tools, chaos gear and production balancing are later work.
