# PvP and Party Activities

> [[00 Start Here|Home]] › [[01 Design Guide|Design Guide]] › **03 Progression and Activities** · Previous: [[Life Skills and Economy]] · Next section: [[Design Questions]]

## Optional PvP

There is no hostile open-world PvP. Players opt into arenas, races, contests, or festival games. The standard mode normalizes combat power and supplies equipment; an optional chaos mode allows personal gear. Rewards are reputation, cosmetics, and modest materials—not exclusive power.

## Party activities

Short 3–10 minute activities live at real places: tavern games, fishing contests, cooking rushes, races, duels, seasonal festivals, and cooperative town tasks. They use the same characters and world rewards.

Activities appear as breaks inside normal play and social gatherings, not as a separate lobby or the main progression grind.
