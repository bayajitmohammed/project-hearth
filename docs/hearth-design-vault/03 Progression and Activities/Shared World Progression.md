# Shared World Progression

> [[00 Start Here|Home]] › [[01 Design Guide|Design Guide]] › **03 Progression and Activities** · Previous: [[Player Progression]] · Next: [[Life Skills and Economy]]

## Question

How does the shared world progress?

## Answer

The group improves a **shared homestead** and earns influence in nearby settlements. Projects unlock practical changes: workshops, gardens, transport links, merchants, defenses, festivals, and new expeditions.

Regions also remember major outcomes. A rescued character may move into town; a repaired bridge opens a route; an ignored threat may temporarily occupy a site. Important changes are recorded in a shared chronicle.

Progress does not reset or decay while people are away. New projects expand choices and identity rather than making old areas useless.
