# Discord

> [[00 Start Here|Home]] › [[01 Design Guide|Design Guide]] › **04 Production** · Decisions: [[Design Questions]]

## Question

What should Discord add?

## Answer

Discord is not required for launch and never hosts gameplay or progression.

A later integration may provide rich presence, join invitations, and opt-in notices for group-created plans or completed shared projects. Notifications are infrequent, configurable, and contain links back into the game. There are no grind commands, Discord-only rewards, or required community-server membership.
