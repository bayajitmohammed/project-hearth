# AI Asset Policy

> [[00 Start Here|Home]] › [[01 Design Guide|Design Guide]] › **04 Production** · Decisions: [[Design Questions]]

## Answer

AI may assist asset creation, but every shipped asset must be intentionally selected, edited, and reviewed by the developer.

- Record the tool, model, date, prompt/source inputs, edits, and commercial-use terms for each asset batch.
- Use only tools and inputs that grant suitable commercial rights.
- Reject recognizable characters, brands, signatures, and close imitation of a living artist's style.
- Review consistency, accessibility, performance, and unintended artifacts by hand.
- Clearly disclose material AI use to storefronts and players; never market the work as entirely handmade.
- Keep live generation out of the initial release. It adds moderation, safety, cost, and policy obligations.

Price reflects the finished experience, reliability, support, and operating cost—not how many manual hours produced each asset. AI lowers production cost but does not excuse weak quality or unclear ownership.
